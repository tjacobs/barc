# Welcome to BARC: 🤖 Bay Area Robot Club 🤖

A club for people who build robots. In-person meetups in Palo Alto, hardware demos, online discussion, and research collaboration.

## Web

https://tjacobs.github.io/barc

## What we're about

Robots, actuators, hardware, software. Show what you've built, find collaborators, keep up with what's new in robotics, hardware, and machine learning.

## Meetups

Last Sunday of every month. Next: **Sunday, September 27th, 2026, 1–2.30pm** — Lightberry, Dogpatch, San Francisco.

## Access

Post in #introductions: who you are, what area you're in/university, what you've built (photos/video welcome), what you're working on now, and you'll be granted full Builder role posting access.

## Tips

**Show your work.** Have a video or photos in #show-and-tell posts.
**Be technical.** Talk about the details of what you or your collaborators have built.

## Start

Glad you're here. Now go post in #introductions. 👇
