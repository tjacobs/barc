# Welcome to BARC — Bay Area Robot Club 🤖

**A club for people who build robots.** In-person meetups in Palo Alto, hardware demos, online discussion, and research collaboration.

🌐 https://tjacobs.github.io/barc/

## What we're about

Robots, actuators, hardware, software. Show what you've built, get unblocked, find collaborators, keep up with what's new.

## Meetups

**Last Sunday of every month** in Palo Alto. First one: **June 28th, 2026.** Details in #meetups.

## Roles

- **Guest** — where everyone starts. You can read everything and chat in GENERAL.
- **Builder** — full access, including posting in BUILDS and RESEARCH & TECH. You become a Builder when an existing Builder vouches for you, or when you share your work.

**Want the Builder role?** Post in #introductions: who you are, what you've built (photos/video welcome), what you're working on now.

## Ground rules

1. **Show your work.** #show-and-tell posts need an image or video.
2. **Be technical, be generous.** Detailed answers in #build-help make this place worth being in.
3. **One thread per project** in #build-logs — keep the long journeys readable.
4. **No spam, no self-promo without substance.** Selling something? It better be a robot part we'd actually want.

Glad you're here. Now go post in #introductions. 👇
